## Launch a Sub‑Second Store That Converts 2.5× Better on Mobile in 90 Days

For Shopify brands stuck at 2–3% conversion on mobile, hitting theme limits, or needing omnichannel flexibility that monolithic can’t deliver.

### The Problem

Your Shopify theme is a cage. Mobile conversion lags desktop by 50–70% because your theme loads 3–5 seconds on 4G and can’t adapt to your custom pricing, bundling, or content logic. Developers fight Liquid instead of shipping features, and every “customization” is a brittle hack that breaks on the next Shopify update.

Headless commerce fixes this: offload the frontend to a modern framework (Next.js, Remix) and treat Shopify as your secure checkout and product API. Brands that make the leap see 2.5× higher mobile conversion and 30% faster developer velocity—but most agencies quote 6–12 month projects that never ship.

### How It Works

1. **Audit & Architecture** – Map current theme, identify performance bottlenecks, design headless stack (Next.js/Remix, Vercel/Netlify, Shopify GraphQL)
2. **Build & Integrate** – Develop new storefront, connect Shopify Checkout, rebuild custom logic (pricing, bundles, CMS) as API‑first services
3. **Migrate & Test** – Zero‑downtime switchover, SEO preservation (301s, meta tags), cross‑browser/device QA, load testing
4. **Launch & Optimize** – Deploy, monitor real‑user performance, iterate on conversion‑blocking UX, hand off to your team
