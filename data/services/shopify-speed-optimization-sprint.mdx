            <li>Optimize web fonts (Shopify often loads 3+ font families unnecessarily)</li>

            <li>Fix Interaction to Next Paint (INP): Site responds quickly to clicks</li>
            <li>Fix Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS): Nothing jumps around unexpectedly</li>
            <li>Test across 12+ devices (iPhone, Android, tablets, desktop)</li>
            <li>Simulate slow 4G and 3G connections (real customer conditions)</li>
